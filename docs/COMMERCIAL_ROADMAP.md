## 2. Tier Breakdown

### Tier 1: Community Edition (Free / Open Source)
- **License:** MIT License.
- **Target:** Developers, hobbyists, students, open-source contributors.
- **Features:** Self-hosted Gateway, Local Web UI, multi-provider model harness, basic connectors, trilingual natural language interaction.

### Tier 2: AI Ramu Pro (Commercial License)
- **Target:** Freelancers, digital creators, small business owners in India.
- **Value Proposition:** Packaged single-installer executable (EXE/DMG), automated daily background schedules, pre-trained Indian business templates (GST, e-Way bills, quote generation).

### Tier 3: Business & Enterprise
- **Target:** Indian SMEs, accounting firms, and legal/ops departments.
- **Value Proposition:** Multi-agent collaboration, ERP connector sync (Zoho, TallyPrime), audited corporate access boundaries, and dedicated onboarding support.
