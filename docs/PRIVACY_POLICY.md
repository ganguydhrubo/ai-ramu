# AI RAMU Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Product:** AI RAMU  
**Owner:** `ganguydhrubo`  

---

## 1. Local-First Sovereignty

- AI RAMU is designed as a local-first software application. Your files, documents, task histories, and personal notes remain stored on your own hardware.
- AI RAMU does not run background tracking, user profiling, or unauthorized telemetry.

---

## 2. Model Provider Interactions

- When you instruct AI RAMU to generate text using a third-party model (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq), your prompt and immediate conversation history are transmitted to the configured provider API under that provider's privacy terms.
- You can completely prevent external network calls by selecting the **Local Ollama** provider mode, which processes all inference locally on your computer.

---

## 3. Transparency & Memory Control

- All facts stored by AI RAMU are visible in the **Memory & Privacy** tab in the Control Dashboard.
- You have the absolute right to inspect, selectively delete, or permanently purge all stored memory at any time with a single click.

---

## 4. Third-Party Integrations

- If you connect WhatsApp, Telegram, or Google Workspace, tokens are stored locally on your machine.
- AI RAMU uses official OAuth flows for Google services and never asks for or stores your account password.
